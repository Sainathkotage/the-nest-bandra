OUR PHILOSOPHY

A slower pace in the heart of Bandra.

Tucked away along bustling Hill Road, The Nest was born from a simple yearning: to create a sanctuary where time softens, conversations linger, and every plate feels like home.

Whether you are sinking into an armchair with your morning cold brew, sharing dim sums with childhood friends, or working on your next passion project — you belong here.

🌾 Artisanal Sourdough Fermented for 36 hours and baked daily with French butter.
☕ Single Origin Brews Sourced ethically from shade-grown estates in Chikmagalur.
🐾 Pet Friendly Our verandah welcomes your furry companions with fresh treats.
🌱 Inclusive Kitchen Dedicated Jain, Vegan, and gluten-conscious preparations.
