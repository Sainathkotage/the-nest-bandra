# The Nest Bandra — Cafe Mockup Website

An exact, 1:1 pixel-faithful mockup website for **The Nest Bandra** (*"Feels Like Home"*), located on Hill Road, Bandra West, Mumbai.

Built directly from the design mockup with pure HTML5, CSS3, and lightweight JavaScript.

---

## 🌟 Sections & Features

1. **Hero & Integrated Navigation**:
   - Logo: `the nest` + `FEELS LIKE HOME`
   - Nav items: `Home` (active indicator), `Menu`, `Experiences`, `Events`, `About`, `Contact`
   - Actions: `Order / Reserve` pill button & Shopping Cart icon
   - Hero Headline: `Your Second Home.`
   - Hero Beverage: Layered cold brew with handwritten *"Good Coffee Brighter Days"*
   - Slider indicator `01 — 02 03` & `— HILL ROAD — BANDRA —`
2. **Our Menu**:
   - Header with cursive handwritten sub-badge *"good food. good mood"* & `View Full Menu →`
   - Category filter pills: `All`, `Coffee`, `Breakfast`, `Sandwiches`, `Bowls`, `Desserts`, `Beverages`
   - 8 Signature Items (4-column grid):
     - `Iced Latte` (₹ 220)
     - `Hazelnut Cold Brew` (₹ 240)
     - `Caramel Macchiato` (₹ 250)
     - `Matcha Latte` (₹ 260)
     - `Spanish Latte` (₹ 240)
     - `Classic Cold Coffee` (₹ 220)
     - `Mocha` (₹ 260)
     - `Affogato` (₹ 280)
   - Centered `Load More ↓` button
3. **Featured Banner ("NEW AT THE NEST")**:
   - Dark olive green backdrop
   - `Pistachio Iced Latte` with `Try It Now →` button
   - Shot with crushed pistachios & cursive *"Small Sips Happier Days"*
4. **Experiences (3 Cards)**:
   - `Breakfast` — *"Bright mornings, better days."* → `Explore →`
   - `Workshops` — *"Learn. Create. Belong."* → `See Upcoming →`
   - `Celebrations` — *"Make it memorable at The Nest."* → `Enquire Now →`
5. **Footer / Info Bar**:
   - `Hill Road, Bandra` | Mumbai, Maharashtra 400050
   - `Open Daily` | 11:00 AM – 1:30 AM
   - `Follow Us` | @thenestbandra
   - Elegant signature script: *"Good People Better Coffee"*
